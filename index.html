Three ways in

One application, three sources of capital.

Member cheques

Members run diligence together and then invest individually in the companies they believe in. Small cheques that add up to a round.

Focus cohort

Each year we pick one bottleneck, from industrial heat to grid interconnection, and fund two to four companies working on it.

Annual fund

At the close of diligence, fund members vote to allocate pooled capital on top of the individual cheques already committed.

FAQ

Your questions, answered.

What sectors do you actually invest in?

The common thread is not a sector, it is measurable abatement. The portfolio spans generation and storage, the grid, transport, the built environment, agriculture and food systems, water, ocean health, carbon removal, critical minerals, and the industrial processes behind all of them.

Treat that as a sampling rather than a boundary. If your company succeeding would move real tonnes and you are early enough for angel capital to matter, apply rather than ruling yourself out.

What stage do you fund?

Pre-seed through Series A, with seed as the sweet spot. We invest early on purpose, because the first cheques are the hardest to raise and often the ones that give other investors the confidence to follow.

Can I meet the team before applying?

Not before you apply. We get far more applications than we have capacity to meet, so the application is where every conversation starts.

If your company is easier to explain in person, make the application carry that conversation. The insight behind the product, the traction that does not fit on a slide, and why your team is the one to build it all belong in the deck.

How long does it take?

Cheques can land four to six weeks after you pitch. It depends on the time of year, how well your data room is put together, and how quickly conversations move. We will not drag it out either way.

What should I prepare for the pitch?

Ten minutes, and clarity beats polish. The pitches that land usually cover:

  • The problem: who has it and what it costs them today
  • The solution: what you have built, why it wins, and why now
  • Traction: pilots, customers, or whatever evidence exists that this works
  • The model: how you make money and how investors see a return
  • The team: why you are the people to build it
  • Impact: the abatement, in units you can defend
  • The ask: how much, on what terms, and what milestones it buys

Leave room for questions. That is usually where conviction gets built.

Is there any cost to apply?

No. We do not charge companies fees at any stage of the process.
